About us

Your trusted logistics partner

TKA Logistics was founded in 2004 in Krnjevo, near Velika Plana, as a small family business specializing in the transport of goods between Serbia and Western Europe.

Our story

From a family company to a regional partner

Over time, thanks to our commitment to quality and innovation, we have grown into a leader in connecting the Western Balkans with the rest of the world.

Our mission is to provide reliable and efficient logistics solutions that meet the needs of our clients. With a strong presence in central Serbia, south of Belgrade, we continuously work on improving our services to become your first choice for all logistics needs.

Our network

Proud members of Paneco, ELVIS and IFA

As co-owners and exclusive partner for the Western Balkans, we access a pan-European transport network linking members across nearly all European countries. Paneco uses an integrated IT platform for real-time shipment tracking and connects over 30 countries and more than 20 partners.

With more than 250 partners, over 280 locations, 19,000 transport units and 3 million m² of warehouse space, E.L.V.I.S. covers Western and Central Europe. The Germany–Serbia groupage line has operated since July 2020, with average transit of 4–7 working days to our customs warehouse in Dobanovci.

With a global network of logistics partners, IFA enables reliable international forwarding, access to modern technologies and cooperation with leading companies across Europe. Beyond these alliances, we also work with partners in Asia, Africa and North America.
