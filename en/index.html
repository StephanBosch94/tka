Our network

Global partners, local expertise

As co-owners and exclusive partner for the Western Balkans, we access a pan-European network linking members across nearly all European countries. An integrated IT platform enables real-time shipment tracking, while a network of over 30 countries and more than 20 partners ensures fast, reliable delivery across Europe.

With more than 250 partners, over 280 locations, 19,000 transport units and 3 million m² of warehouse space, E.L.V.I.S. covers Western and Central Europe. Our Germany – Serbia groupage line has operated since 2020, with average transit of 4–7 business days to our customs warehouse in Dobanovci.

Through a global network of logistics partners, IFA enables reliable international forwarding. Membership brings access to modern technologies, industry practices, and cooperation with leading logistics companies across Europe — support for complex cross-border shipments.

The quality of our services is evidenced by numerous references from various industries, such as:

  • Automotive industry
  • Metal industry
  • Construction industry

  • Chemical industry
