Services

International road transport

Through membership in the Paneco, ELVIS and IFA alliances, and cooperation with transport companies across Europe, we offer groupage (LTL), partial (PTL) and full truckload (FTL) services, including ADR and temperature-controlled cargo.

Send inquiry Learn more

Groupage transport (LTL)

Less-than-Truckload combines smaller shipments from different clients into one truck, reducing costs and maximizing capacity. Ideal for smaller quantities of goods.

Through the E.L.V.I.S. network we offer competitive prices and frequent departures from Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, the United Kingdom, France, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Finland to Serbia and the Western Balkans. Paneco covers consolidated shipments throughout Europe.

Partial truckload (PTL)

PTL is for shipments larger than LTL that do not fill an entire truck. Unlike groupage, the shipment stays on the same vehicle throughout the journey.

Full truckload (FTL)

FTL is the fastest, most direct delivery for large volumes — no transshipment, lower risk of damage. We organize FTL transports across Europe.
