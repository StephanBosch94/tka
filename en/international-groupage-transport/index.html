International groupage transport

LTL combines smaller shipments from different clients into one truck — economical and reliable.

Send inquiry Learn more

Through the E.L.V.I.S. network we offer competitive prices and frequent departures from Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, the United Kingdom, France, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Finland to Serbia and the Western Balkans. Paneco covers consolidated shipments throughout Europe.

With more than 250 partners, over 280 locations, 19,000 transport units and 3 million m² of warehouse space, the network covers Western and Central Europe.

The Germany–Serbia groupage line has operated since July 2020. Shipments collected to the E.L.V.I.S. HUB in Knüllwald are transported twice a week to our customs warehouse in Dobanovci, with average transit of 4–7 working days from pickup.
